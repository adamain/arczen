# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-14)

First public release.

- **Appearance:** rounded shapes, sidebar blended into the window, page laid out as a card, adjustable scales and border thickness.
- **Command bar:** centered panel, blurred or opaque, with adjustable opacity and blur.
- **Site menu:** configurable 4th button.
- **Extensions:** pinned extensions as tiles under the address bar.
- **Tabs:** Tidy sorts tabs into sections, locally by topic or by site, or by intent with Claude (optional, with your own API key).
- **Sidebar:** discreet scrollbar, cleaner titles, collapsed-folder preview, Gmail unread preview.
- **Links and downloads:** link preview, Little Arc, renaming of downloads with unreadable names.
- **Picture-in-Picture:** automatic opening, animated entrance, throw to a corner.

Tested on Zen 1.24.1b with Sine 2.3.0.3, on Windows 11.
